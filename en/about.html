About the podcast

“Without Armor” is a series of conversations about people, war and faith that help people with different experiences of war hear one another.

A space where we try to speak openly but without cynicism, to look for hope without falseness, and to talk about people, faith and war — without armor.

The podcast was conceived by Oleksandr Kyliushyk. It is a collaboration between the Ukrainian Baptist Theological Seminary and the mission Ukraine for Christ.

I want to preserve soldiers' stories about the war. Stories can be therapeutic both for those who tell them and for those who listen.

Oleksandr Kyliushyk

What we talk about

Our guests are veterans and soldiers, combat medics, chaplains, pastors, military wives and relatives of the fallen. We ask about faith at the front, about returning to church and civilian life, about loss, fear and calling, and about how a community can stand alongside someone who has been through war.

The episodes are recorded in Ukrainian. On this site you can read the full English text of every conversation.
