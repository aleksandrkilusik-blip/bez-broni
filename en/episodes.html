04 Episode 4 · June 11, 2026

“God can comfort the heart in a way no person can”

Guest: Denys Ivashchenko

Denys is a veteran of the Armed Forces of Ukraine with the call sign “Chaplain,” an evangelical believer. Addiction in his youth, conversion, ministry to at-risk teenagers, mobilization, training in Britain, the “Skelia” regiment, and being wounded on his first combat mission.
